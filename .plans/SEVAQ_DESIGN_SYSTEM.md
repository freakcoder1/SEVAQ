# SEVAQ DESIGN SYSTEM

## Brand Personality

SevaQ is:

* Premium
* Trustworthy
* Professional
* Managed
* Calm
* Human

SevaQ is NOT:

* Playful
* Cartoonish
* Marketplace-like
* Food-delivery-like
* Overly corporate

---

## Design Principles

1. Trust before conversion
2. Simplicity before features
3. Clarity before decoration
4. Consistency over creativity
5. Operational excellence

## Trust Components

Allowed:

✓ Service Guarantee
✓ Verified Professional
✓ Replacement Support
✓ Quality Monitoring
✓ Flexible Scheduling

Avoid:

✗ Marketing hype
✗ Fake urgency
✗ Excessive badges

---
